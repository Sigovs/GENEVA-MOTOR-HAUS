## carfax.svg (added 2026-09-16)
The "CARFAX · Vehicle History Reports" badge, byte-for-byte from the Chicago Motor Cars preview (`Chicago Motor Cars/assets/logos/carfax.svg`), which took it from CMC's own AAN theme. Artwork for a dark ground (its lower line is white). Used on every car page under the gallery, linking to AAN's Carfax report URL for that VIN (`carfax.com/cfm/ccc_displayhistoryrpt.cfm?partner=AAN_0&vin=…`). Carfax's trademark; never redrawn.
